# "Travails of a Mother" and Dr. Eunice: Web Research Report

Research date: 2026-10-06. Every fact below carries a source URL. "Unverified" means a claim seen only in a search-engine summary or in an inference I made, not confirmed on a primary page. "Inferred" means I worked it out from dates or wording; it is not stated anywhere.

---

## 0. Key finding: which program this is, and which assumption was wrong

- The program is **not Nigerian or Ghanaian, and it is not a maternal-health or medical program.** "Travails of a Mother" is a **Christian prayer and intercession program for mothers** in **East Africa (Uganda and Kenya)**. Mothers are equipped to pray ("travail") for their children.
- The organiser is **Dr. Eunice Adubango (PhD)**, also written **Dr. Eunice Naigaga Adubango**. She is a Ugandan civil engineer and lecturer turned entrepreneur, Christian teacher and mentor, and is based in Kampala.
  - Source: a third-party video titled "I attended the Travails of a mother Conference organized by Dr. Eunice Adubango." (Baingana Sandra, 1 Mar 2026), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z6zpBX1wOH4
  - Most of the program's own content is on her YouTube channel, https://www.youtube.com/@EuniceAdubango
- **Her PhD is in civil engineering / construction project management, not medicine.** "Dr." is an academic title. Sources: https://www.awlsummit.com/awls2022/ and https://lifesignatures.libsyn.com/1210-eunice-adubango-business-principles-all-believers-should-know
- **The program has almost no presence outside social media.** I found no news articles, no dedicated website, and no NGO or foundation registration for "Travails of a Mother". Nearly all program facts come from YouTube titles and descriptions on Dr. Eunice's channel.

## 2. What "Travails of a Mother" is

### 2.1 Nature and mission (as the program describes itself)

None of these statements are formal mission or vision statements. I could not find any; the client should supply them.

- "There is no telling how blessed a child will turn out when a mother prays! Come, let us get equipped to travail for our children." Source: "Will you be there? 1st edition of travails of a mother #WomenOfPrayer" (30 Dec 2023), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0pEKoxSiDQg
- An attendee describes it as a "Conference organized by Dr. Eunice Adubango where she drew mothers together with a mission to intercede for their children's lives." Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z6zpBX1wOH4 (Baingana Sandra, 1 Mar 2026)
- "Our prayers will change our children's destinies! We got no excuse. We MUST PRAY!" Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MyuZornvgMs
- "Meeting God over our children is a VERY IMPORTANT MATTER!" Source: "Prepare to meet the king:- A word from ladies organizing travails of a mother" (12 Feb 2024), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tgvnqQILN68
- Recurring themes: "Digging wells for generations!" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pGq-kftNnZM); "wells of salvation and dominion that we dug at travials 2025" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q4l2sAHePNU); praying for children "unto the 4th generation" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kY64ucq6HI4). Dr. Eunice calls herself "a repairer of broken wells" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FEHUwwdDezI).
- Background statement: "I share part of my 18year journey of travailing in the presence of God." Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YomjeBwwrOQ (7 Feb 2024)
- Target beneficiaries: mothers, and women praying for children (including women hoping to conceive and mothers of special-needs children, per channel content). The program frames intercession as the mother's role. From "2024 Prayer Strategies for mothers" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kev2H0ZQZK0): "I have a call for every woman to arise and pray for their child in 2024!"

Not found at all: a formal mission or vision statement, founding story beyond the above, legal entity or registration, board, team names, impact report, logo files, or brand colours.

## 5. Methods and limits

- I used many web searches (standard and extended), including all the query variants suggested. Generic web search returned **nothing** for the program; the program was found only through YouTube's own search.
- YouTube data (titles, dates, descriptions, channel stats) was pulled from YouTube's public pages and endpoints. I did not obtain video transcripts or watch the videos, so spoken content (venues, team names, ticket prices) is **unknown**.
- These pages were blocked or behind a login: Daily Monitor (Cloudflare), New Vision (paywall), LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, SSRN, ScienceDirect, Wayback Machine. Facts attributed only to search snippets of these pages are marked unverified.
- No program website was found (travailsofamother.com does not resolve and has no Wayback snapshot).
