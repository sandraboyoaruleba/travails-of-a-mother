About the program

Mothers who pray change destinies

Travails of a Mother is a prayer and intercession gathering convened by Dr. Eunice Adubango. It equips mothers to travail: to labour in prayer for their children, as they once laboured to bring them into the world.

Travails of a Mother is for mothers everywhere, whatever their nation. The first gatherings were held in February 2024, in person and online, and the program has since grown into a worldwide community of praying mums. It meets at an annual gathering, at seasonal prayer marathons and every day on YouTube, so a mother can join from anywhere.

Mothers are taught to pray with strategy and vision: to see their children's future, to pray “unto the fourth generation”, and to dig wells of salvation and blessing that their children and grandchildren will draw from.

“Our prayers will change our children's destinies! We got no excuse. We MUST PRAY!”

Meet the convener

Dr. Eunice Naigaga Adubango

Engineer, lecturer, entrepreneur, author, and above all a praying mother.

Dr. Eunice is a Ugandan civil engineer who holds a PhD in civil engineering project management (institution & year), a Master's in engineering management from the University of Dar es Salaam, and a BSc in Civil Engineering from Makerere University (2002). She lectured at Makerere University for twelve years (2006–2018) and has lectured at the Ndejje University School of Graduate Engineering Studies.

She stepped from academia into business as the owner and managing partner of Eunie's Kitchen Ltd, Talitha Store and Eunie's Culinary School. She is the author of four books, including In Relentless Pursuit: A Devotional for Women, a devotional she writes every year other book titles years published.

Travails of a Mother grew out of her own 18-year journey of travailing in the presence of God for her children. She is married to Eng. Brand Adubango, and together they are parents to three sons.

Our journey

From one gathering to a movement of praying mums

  1. 30 Dec 2023

    The call goes out

    “Will you be there?” The first edition of Travails of a Mother is announced.

  2. Feb 2024

    1st Edition: Kampala

    The first physical gathering. Mothers glimpsed decades into their children's lives and prayed it. Exact date · venue (Naguru Skys?) · attendance

  3. 24 Feb 2024

    1st Edition: Kenya

    Nairobi, online and in person: 280 women resourced. “Words cannot express what the move of God in that room was like.” Venue

  4. Apr & Jul 2024

    The first prayer marathons

    Three-day marathons of scripture-based prayer for children, live on YouTube. They became a quarterly rhythm.

  5. 5 Dec 2024

    70-Day Prayer Marathon

    “IT IS WAR TIME!” Seventy days of prayer covering every woman coming to the 2nd edition and every child she would represent.

  6. Feb 2025

    2nd Edition: “The Battle for Our Children's Birthright and Inheritances”

    Wells of salvation and dominion dug for the generations. Exact date · venue · attendance · theme colour

  7. Nov 2025

    The First-Born Season

    A month of intercession for first-born children and their birthright, with night and early-morning prayer meetings.

  8. Dec 2025

    Teens, and gifts for vulnerable children

    A teaching session and a three-week prayer class for teenagers. The community also raised UGX 2.4 million for gifts to vulnerable children, which were delivered on 13 December.

  9. 28 Dec 2025

    Sold out

    Every ticket for the 3rd edition is taken, almost two months ahead.

  10. Sat 21 Feb 2026

    3rd Edition: Hotel Africana

    Mothers gathered in royal purple. The prophetic word “The Doors are Open” was released over the families represented. Theme · attendance

  11. 2026

    Growing beyond one day

    Mentorship for children and teens, a widows' purse (UGX 7.3 million in May 2026), quarterly marathons, and a weekly 3am prayer watch.

  12. Aug 2026

    410+ tickets for 2027

    More than 410 mothers have paid for the 4th edition six months ahead, in a community of over 1,300 women.

  13. Sat 20 Feb 2027

    4th Edition: “Removing Barriers, Gaining Access, and Unlocking God's Possibilities for Our Children”

    Hotel Africana, Kampala, 6am–6pm. Dress code: Scarlet Red. Get your ticket.
